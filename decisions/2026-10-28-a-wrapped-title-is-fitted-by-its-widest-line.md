## 2026-10-28 — A wrapped title is fitted by its widest line

**Context:** On an iPad Air in portrait (820px wide), „Der Mond ist aufgegangen" sat on its title slide at its natural size, its two lines a third short of the screen edges, while the lyric slides filled the screen. `slide-zoom.js` measured each slide by its box (`scrollWidth`). The lyric slides are `white-space: nowrap`, so their box is their widest line. A title slide's heading wraps instead: once it is too long for one line, its box takes the whole width (it is a shrink-to-fit flex item, so the box is the smaller of the unwrapped title and the space available), while the wrapped lines fall short of it. Measured that way, it was "already full" at scale 1. The deck's own title slide had the same problem at that width. Phones were unaffected: there the box narrows to the longest word, which the lines then fill. Wide screens were unaffected because the title fits on one line.

**Decision:** Keep the browser's own wrapping, and measure a title slide (the deck's, the Introduction's, every song's) by its widest line: a `Range` over each child's contents, whose line rects are divided by the box's rendered-to-layout ratio to get layout pixels. That widest line, not the box, is scaled to the room on screen. On the iPad the song's title stays on two lines and grows to about 1.5×. Lyric slides are measured as before.

**Reasoning:** The alternative was `white-space: nowrap` on title slides too, like the lyric slides. That is one line of CSS and always gives the true width, but it puts every title on a single line, *smaller* than before on a portrait screen (about 61px instead of 80px on the iPad, never mind filling the width), and long titles get tiny on a phone. Fitting by the widest line keeps what the fit is for, "as large as it goes without overflowing", and changes nothing where the title already fit on one line. Measuring lines is safe only because a title slide holds nothing but lines of text (`validate_song!` allows nothing between the H1 and the first H2). A list's markers or a `pre`'s padding would not show up in a `Range`'s rects, which is why the lyric slides keep `scrollWidth`. The lines stay centred in the box (`text-align: center`, and the box is never narrower than its longest word, so no line overflows to one side), so scaling the box about its centre takes them to the edges. `text-wrap: balance` would even out the lines further and was left out to keep the change to the measurement.
