## 2026-10-04 — Guest-volunteer resolution gets a per-recipient toast

**Context:** When a guest volunteers to live-scroll and the presenter names a scroller, the invite dialog on every other invited client just closed (`reconcileSession`'s `else` branch) with no further feedback — a guest who clicked „Ja, ich scrolle" had no way of knowing whether they'd been picked, short of watching the status line change underneath them. The presenter, too, only saw `#multiplex-status` change to „Gast scrollt", with no acknowledgement that a volunteer had come forward at all.

**Decision:** Four short-lived toasts on the existing `#multiplex-toast` element (already used for the end-of-session notice, so no new UI surface), each shown once per session and routed by what *this* client did: the chosen scroller gets „Danke für deine Bereitschaft! Lass uns gleich starten…"; a volunteer who lost the race to someone faster gets „Oh, das wäre nett gewesen – aber jemand anderes scrollt bereits!"; everyone else who was invited (declined, or never answered) gets „Es geht gleich los!"; the presenter gets „Jemand hat sich bereit erklärt, es geht gleich los" the instant it accepts the first volunteer. A latecomer who was never invited for that session sees none of them — the resolution is not their concern.

**Reasoning:** A toast rather than a dialog: none of these four messages need an acknowledgement click, and the existing self-announce dialog's required OK click was deliberately not the model to copy here — the recipient should just be told and get on with watching the song start. Routing by recipient (rather than one generic "a scroller was chosen" message for everyone) costs one extra per-session flag (`volunteeredFor`, mirroring the existing `invitedFor`) but means a guest who took the trouble of volunteering and lost isn't told the same flat line as someone who never answered at all. The transition is detected once per session (`!hadScrollerAlready` in `reconcileSession`), so a later heartbeat repeating the same already-resolved session doesn't re-show it.
