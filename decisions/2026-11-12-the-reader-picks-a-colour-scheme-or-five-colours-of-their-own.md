## 2026-11-12 — The reader picks a colour scheme, or five colours of their own

**Context:** ⚙️ offered a dark and a bright theme, the bright one a `body.theme-bright` class with some thirty hand-picked overrides in `night.css`. Wanted: colours one picks oneself; then no dark/bright mode is needed, rather a dropdown of templates whose colours a colour picker can override.

**Decision:** The „Farben" select offers schemes — Dunkel, Hell, Kontrast, Nachtblau, Lagerfeuer — and „Eigene", with five colour pickers under it: Hintergrund, Text, Titel, Links, Fett. A scheme is those five colours in `night.css`; every other colour on the page is mixed from them with `color-mix()`. Whatever needs to know whether the background is light (the white font samples, the code blocks' paper, the 📡 badge's grey and blue) reads `body.theme-light`, which the script sets from the background's luminance. A colour picked makes the five the reader's own, the other four taken from the scheme shown; a scheme picked afterwards leaves them stored, and „Eigene" brings them back. Kept per device, not synced, like the fonts.

**Reasoning:** Five, because they are every colour of a slide that differed between the two themes (the chord pills and the code blocks' paper did not); fewer would have forced Hell's dark titles and golden links into one colour, more would have meant pickers for the dialogs' greys, which nobody wants to pick. Mixed rather than listed per scheme, because a reader's own colours need their dialogs, borders and artist line too, and a formula is the only thing that can provide them for colours nobody chose in advance; the formulas were tuned to give back Dunkel's previous greys almost exactly. Hell's dialogs therefore turned faintly yellow (from its background) instead of neutral grey, and its artist line a dimmed rather than a darker grey, which was accepted. `color-mix()` in CSS rather than mixing in JavaScript: it keeps the colours in the stylesheet. It came with Safari 16.2 and Chrome 111 (2023); an older browser shows the slides' five colours all the same, and only loses the mixed ones (a dialog without its own background, over the dimmed page), which was accepted. The schemes' colours live only in `night.css`; the pickers read them back from the computed style, so there is no second copy in the script. „Eigene" stays its own entry in the select rather than marking a scheme as changed („Hell (angepasst)"): one can try a scheme and return to one's own without losing it. Light or dark is decided from the background alone, by the WCAG luminance threshold, rather than kept as a second setting the reader would have to get right.
