## 2026-10-04 — Scroller's Space/Shift+Space remap to `Reveal.down()`/`up()` instead of being disabled

**Context:** [Yesterday's fix](2026-10-03-about-slide-merges-instructions-and-resources-tags-split-into-sprache-genre.md) added Space (and Home/End) to `SCROLLER_KEYS` with a `null` binding, to stop reveal.js's default Space handling — `next()`/`prev()` — from stepping the live-scroll scroller past the end of its song into the neighbouring one, losing its scroll position when the snap-back corrected it (v reset to 0). That worked, but as a side effect it also made Space do nothing at all while scrolling — including the ordinary case of just advancing through the song, which is what most of a scroller's Space presses are actually for. A user testing the guest-scroll feature noticed and flagged it as broken.

**Decision:** `SCROLLER_KEYS[32]` is now a function, not `null`: `function(e) { e.shiftKey ? Reveal.up() : Reveal.down(); }`. Home and End stay `null` — fully disabled, unchanged.

**Reasoning:** reveal.js's `keyboard` config accepts either `null` (swallow the key) or a function (run instead of the default binding) for each key code; the fix only needed the latter. `Reveal.down()`/`Reveal.up()` were the obvious target to delegate to, rather than re-implementing "step within the song" by hand: the vertical arrow keys already call exactly those two functions for this project's slide structure (horizontal songs each holding vertical sub-slides, default `navigationMode`) and are already relied on as the scroller's safe way to move — see `applyNavMode`'s comment in `style/body-controls.html`. Reusing them means the Space fix inherits that key's already-verified safety instead of needing its own. Home/End were left alone: jumping to the first/last *horizontal* slide has no useful vertical analogue to remap to, so full disabling remains the right call there — only Space's remap, not Home/End's, needed revisiting.
