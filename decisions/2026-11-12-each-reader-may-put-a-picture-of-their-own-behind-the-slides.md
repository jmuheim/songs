## 2026-11-12 — Each reader may put a picture of their own behind the slides

**Context:** Wanted: a background picture one can upload, kept in one's own browser only, with no server behind it.

**Decision:** ⚙️ gets a button „Hintergrund", which opens a dialog of its own: „Bild wählen …" (the device's file picker) and „Entfernen". The picture is shrunk in the browser to 2048 px on its longer side, kept as a JPEG data URL in `localStorage`, and shown on a layer fixed behind the slides, under a veil of the theme's background colour, its strength a slider („Schleier", 0–90%, 70% unless set). Per device, like the theme and the fonts; not synced over multiplex, not in `print.html`.

**Reasoning:** `localStorage` rather than IndexedDB: it can be read synchronously while the page is parsed, so the picture is there from the first frame, as the theme and the fonts are, and the code stays as small as theirs. Its few megabytes are why the picture is shrunk: a phone's photo is tens of megabytes as pixels and several as a file, too large for localStorage; 2048 px on the longer side, at JPEG quality 0.8, comes to a few hundred kilobytes, and under the veil a sharper picture would show no difference. Shrinking also spares a phone keeping a 12- or 24-megapixel photo decoded behind every slide. The veil was fixed at first, for readability on any picture (a bright, busy picture stayed readable at 70%) and for room in a dialog already tight on a phone held sideways; a slider was asked for, since how much veil a picture needs depends on the picture. One value for both themes rather than one each: the reader sets it for their picture, and two sliders would be one too many. A slider is no use if one can't see what it does, and a dialog's backdrop darkens the slides: so the background's dialog has none, and the slides around it show the veil as it moves. Making the dialog step aside while the slider moved, all but the slider, was tried and was more than needed. A dialog of its own rather than a row in the settings: the settings, with the colour schemes and their pickers that came the same day, filled a phone held sideways to within a few pixels, and the background's row, with its veil and parallax, would have made them scroll there. It follows the „Lieder von" dialog, which the table of contents' filter bar got for the same reason (see [Gastgeber get a dialog of their own](2026-10-31-gastgeber-get-a-dialog-of-their-own-songs-von.md)); its button sits in the settings' last row, beside „Schliessen", where it costs no height. The veil and the parallax checkbox show only with a picture. A layer of its own rather than `body`'s background: on an iPhone in Vollbild the page is many screens tall and `background-attachment: fixed` is ignored, so the picture would scroll away. The table of contents painted its background colour in two places that would now show as a band and a column over the picture: the sticky filter bar, which wears the veil and blurs what scrolls under it instead, and the overlays that fade out a long title at the row's end, which give way to the title's own letters fading (`background-clip: text`). A mask on the link was rejected again for the reason it was the first time: it cuts off the link's focus ring. Without a picture the table of contents stays exactly as it was.
