## 2026-10-04 — Song filenames are slugs, the H1 splits into title and artist

**Context:** Every song file was named `<Title> (<Artist>).md`, duplicating the H1 verbatim, parentheses and all — awkward in a shell, in URLs, and inconsistent with the rest of the repo's lowercase, underscore-separated filenames. "Traditional"/"Traditionell" also sat in that parenthetical as if it were a real credited artist for ten folk songs with no actual one.

**Decision:** Filenames are now a slug derived from the H1: lowercase, `.`/`,`/`'` dropped, German umlauts transliterated (`ä`→`ae`, `ö`→`oe`, `ü`→`ue`, `ß`→`ss`), remaining spaces turned into underscores, everything else (`&`, `!`) passed through as-is — e.g. `# Family Portrait - P!nk` → `family_portrait_-_p!nk.md`, `# Knockin' on Heaven's Door - Bob Dylan` → `knockin_on_heavens_door_-_bob_dylan.md`, `# Über den Wolken - Reinhard Mey` → `ueber_den_wolken_-_reinhard_mey.md`. The H1 itself keeps its real spelling, umlauts included — only the filename is transliterated — and moves from `Title (Artist)` to `Title - Artist` — the literal separator is a space, a hyphen, a space, now reserved (no title may contain that exact substring; an unspaced hyphen like `74-75` is unaffected). A song with no specific artist to credit (`Traditional`/`Traditionell`) drops the artist entirely, from both the H1 and the filename — just `Title` — rather than keeping a placeholder; any annotation that used to ride along in the parenthetical (a few of these carried a 🇨🇭 flag) is dropped with it. `content/legacy-songs/` got the same treatment for consistency, even though it isn't built.

On each song's own title slide, `split_song_artist` (`lib/build_helpers.rb`) splits that H1 back apart at build time: the title stays in the `<h1>`, the artist becomes a sibling `<p class="song-artist">`, styled smaller and a shade darker than the title in both themes. A bare `Title`-only H1 is left untouched.

**Reasoning:** Deriving the filename from the H1 (rather than inventing a second naming scheme) keeps one source of truth — the H1 already is the canonical title, so the filename has nothing new to get wrong or drift out of sync with. Stripping sentence punctuation (`.`/`,`/`'`) but keeping stylistic characters (`!` in `P!nk`, `&` in band names) was a deliberate line: those three are the "a sentence needs this" marks, not part of how a name is actually spelled, and they were the ones making `ls` and shell completion awkward (`S.O.B.`, `Gimme hope, Jo'anna`). Umlauts get the same ASCII-friendly treatment, but transliterated (`ä`→`ae`) rather than dropped (`ä`→`a`), the standard German fallback spelling for exactly this situation — keyboards and systems that can't type or sort `ä` — rather than one this book invented. Splitting the H1 for display, rather than authoring title and artist as two separate fields, avoided adding a second piece of per-song front matter for one rendering tweak — the H1 already carries both, so the build just has to look at it in two pieces instead of one.
