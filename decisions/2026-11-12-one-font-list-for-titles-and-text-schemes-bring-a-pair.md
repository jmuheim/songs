## 2026-11-12 — One font list for titles and text; schemes bring a pair

**Context:** ⚙️ had two lists, „Titelschrift" and „Textschrift", each with its own fonts: a script face was offered for the titles only. Wanted: a single list, every font for the titles and for the text alike, more fonts (easier reading ones like Atkinson Hyperlegible, and fun ones: symbols as on old Windows, outlines, hieroglyphs), and more colour schemes, which might carry fonts of their own.

**Decision:** One list, a font to a row, with a radio button for the titles and one for the text in each row; twenty fonts, every one for either. Its first row, „Passend zum Thema", is the colour scheme's own pair (`--theme-title-font`/`--theme-text-font` in `night.css`): Papier brings Playfair Display and Lora, Terminal VT323, Kunterbunt the colour font Nabla and Comic Relief, and so on. A font the reader picks stays across every scheme. „Eigene" keeps the fonts of the scheme it was made from. Added: Lexend and OpenDyslexic for reading, VT323, Londrina Outline, Nabla, and two fonts built here from Noto, „Liedli Symbole" and „Liedli Hieroglyphen", whose letters are pictures. A scheme may also go beyond colours and fonts: Graustufen greys out the whole page with a filter.

**Reasoning:** Two radio buttons in one row rather than one choice for both: titles and lyrics read differently, and a pair is half of what makes a scheme. The earlier decision kept script faces out of the text list because they tire over a whole song's lyrics; offering them anyway trusts the reader, and the scheme's pair is a sensible default. A scheme picks the fonts only while the reader hasn't: otherwise someone who needs Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic to read would lose it with every scheme they try. A scheme that *set* both fonts on being picked was rejected for that reason, and so was keeping fonts out of the schemes, which left Terminal or Kunterbunt in Montserrat. Wingdings itself may not be shipped, and the open symbol and hieroglyph fonts map their pictures to Unicode's own code points, not to letters, so typing lyrics in them shows nothing; remapping letters to their glyphs, as the OFL allows under another name, was the only way to get the joke. The ids sit on `<body>` either way (the reader's pick or the scheme's), so `night.css` has one rule per font and use, and each family stack is written once. Headings ask for weight 700 with `font-synthesis: style`, so a font that has a bold shows it and one that hasn't isn't smeared into a fake one. The chord pills in the lyrics now wear the text's font too, rather than staying monospace: they are part of the line, and nothing in a pill needs to line up. The fingerings in code blocks and in the chord tooltip do, and stay monospace. The pills follow even a picture font, where „Am" becomes an aeroplane and a fish: keeping them monospace there was tried and dropped, since whoever picks hieroglyphs for a joke picks them for the chords too.
