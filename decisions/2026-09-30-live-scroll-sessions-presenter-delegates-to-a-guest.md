## 2026-09-30 — Live-scrolling is a presenter-led session, optionally handed to a guest

**Context:** The old multiplex model had a „master" whose every slide move was broadcast continuously, with clients following or browsing freely (👣). It was leaderless — each tab ran a claim / leader-silence protocol to agree who led. We wanted a different shape: the presenter (the guitarist) explicitly starts a *live-scroll session* on the current song, and either scrolls it themselves or hands the scrolling to a volunteer from the audience for that one song.

**Decision:** The presenter is now the single authority. 🚀 (password-gated, once per tab) opens a choice — „Ich selber möchte scrollen" or „Ein Gast soll scrollen". In guest mode every client is asked „… Möchtest du das Live-Scrollen übernehmen?"; the first „Ja" wins and leads the room. Whoever scrolls — presenter or guest — is locked to the session's song (vertical only; horizontal navigation snaps back). Everyone else follows with keyboard, touch and controls disabled (no more 👣 opt-out). „Live-Scrollen beenden" ends it, with a toast „Du kannst jetzt wieder frei navigieren" to all and „Vielen Dank fürs Scrollen!" on top for the guest.

Three message types ride on the one relayed event: `session` (from the presenter), `state` (from the scroller), `volunteer` (from a client). The presenter heartbeats the session and the scroller its position, so latecomers catch up; a latecomer who arrives before a guest is chosen is invited too. The relay is unchanged — it still only checks `sha256(secret) == socketId` and forwards the whole payload, so no server change was needed.

**Reasoning:** A single authority removes almost all of the leaderless machinery: the presenter alone opens/closes a session and names the scroller, so two volunteers cannot both take over (the presenter picks the first it hears). Only a small `claim`/`from` rule survives, to settle a presenter that reloads or is duplicated. Song-locking the scroller keeps a handed-off guest from wandering the whole set list. Enforced following (no 👣) was chosen over an opt-out so the room stays together during a song; a follower that hears nothing from the session for three heartbeats frees itself rather than freeze, and the presenter's next heartbeat locks it again.

One build wrinkle surfaced: `post_process_index` rewrites Pandoc's `keyboard: true,` (to disable the „s" speaker-notes key), and the new code legitimately calls `Reveal.configure({ keyboard: true, … })` when a session ends. `body-controls.html` is now spliced in *after* those rewrites so the sub targets Pandoc's init, not ours.
