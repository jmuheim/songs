## 2026-09-30 — The presenter role survives a reload; whoever took over last leads

**Context:** The presenter role was lost on every reload, silently: the repeats stopped and nobody followed anyone any more, which is what a first manual test ran into. Keeping the role in `sessionStorage` fixes that, but a duplicated tab inherits it — and the relay stops nobody from sending, while the password ships in the page.

**Decision:** The role is kept per tab, with its claim, and restored without the password. Every message carries `claim` (the time of the takeover) and `from` (an id per page load). A presenter that hears a higher claim steps down and follows; a takeover claims at least one above every claim it heard. Between two presenters with an equal claim, the higher `from` stays. A client ignores a lower claim until the leader has been silent for 65 s, and takes an equal claim as the same leader reloaded.

**Reasoning:** This is the core of Axipedia's rule without its trimmings (step-down by reload, messages in a pill, a log, a rival warning). It was first left out as „too much"; that was wrong — once the role survives a reload, a duplicated tab makes two presenters, and the rule is what settles it. The claim-plus-one keeps a device whose clock runs behind from losing a fresh takeover. Clients must not compare ids on an equal claim: a reloaded presenter gets a new id and would be ignored half the time. The id tie-break only matters when two messages cross in flight, which a browser spec cannot stage reliably; a spec shows that of two duplicated tabs exactly one stays presenter.
