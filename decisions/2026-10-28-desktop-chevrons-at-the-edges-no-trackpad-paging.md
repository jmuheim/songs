## 2026-10-28 — Desktop chevrons at the screen edges, no trackpad paging

**Context:** A friend opened the song book on a laptop, swiped on the trackpad, and nothing happened, so they thought the navigation was broken. Two things added up. Reveal's chevrons had been switched off everywhere back in June (`controls: false`), so a desktop showed nothing to click. And Reveal only handles swipes from a touch screen. A trackpad sends `wheel` events, and nothing listened to those.

**Decision:** The chevrons are back on desktops, in Reveal's `edges` layout, and hidden under `@media (pointer: coarse), (width < 500px)`. `slide-zoom.js` fits each slide between the side chevrons. Trackpad swipes and the mouse wheel still don't page the deck. The chevrons and the arrow keys are the desktop's way through the book.

**Reasoning:**
- **Edges, not the default bottom-right cluster:** the cluster sits exactly where 🚀 is. Below 500px, reveal.css itself drops the edges layout and falls back to that cluster, which is why narrow windows hide the chevrons as well.
- **`pointer: coarse` rather than `hover: hover`:** headless Chrome on the Linux CI runner reports no hover (the same trap the tooltip specs hit), so a hover gate would hide the chevrons on the "desktop" the specs test.
- **Fitting between the chevrons:** a scan of the real book at desktop width found 407 of 697 slides whose zoomed content reached into a chevron's band. Without the gutter, lyrics would sit under the arrows. The gutter is measured from the button's layout offsets, because Reveal animates the chevrons with a `transform` and `getBoundingClientRect` gave a different width depending on when it ran. Width-limited slides now zoom about 7% smaller at 1280px; the pinned zoom values in the slide-zoom spec changed by exactly that ratio (1192/1280).
- **Trackpad paging was built, then dropped.** A `wheel` handler that paged one slide per gesture worked in the specs but never felt reliable on a real MacBook. The core problem is telling a swipe's momentum from the next swipe: macOS keeps sending momentum events for a second or two after the fingers lift, and no web API exposes the trackpad's phases (finger down, momentum). Ending a gesture after a 250ms pause made a second swipe during the momentum do nothing. Detecting the next swipe from rising deltas fixed that case in the specs, but still felt flaky in real use. Reveal's own `mouseWheel` option is no alternative: it calls `next()`/`prev()` at most once a second, so one momentum swipe skips two or three slides; and it would page the deck instead of scrolling the table of contents. A guess that sometimes skips a slide or ignores a swipe is worse than visible chevrons that always work.
