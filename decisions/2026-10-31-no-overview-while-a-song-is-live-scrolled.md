## 2026-10-31 — No overview while a song is live-scrolled

**Context:** A host scrolling a song itself could still open Reveal's overview with Esc or O. It showed the neighbouring songs, which the scroller can't reach: a click on one jumped there, and the snap-back returned it to its song. The overview was kept for the presenter in [the overview and a pause stay on the presenter's screen](2026-09-30-overview-and-pause-stay-on-the-presenters-screen.md), from a time when the presenter's every move was mirrored and the overview was how it looked for the next song. Since [live-scroll sessions](2026-09-30-live-scroll-sessions-presenter-delegates-to-a-guest.md), whoever scrolls is locked to one song, and the next song is found between live scrolls, with nothing locked.

**Decision:** While a song is live-scrolled, the overview is off (`overview: false`) for the scroller, host or guest, and for every follower; it comes back once the live scroll ends. One still open as live scrolling starts is closed first. This replaces the part of the earlier entry that had the scroller repeat the slide it opened the overview on: with no overview, there is nothing to repeat. A pause (B) still stays on the scroller's own screen.

**Reasoning:** For someone locked to one song, the overview's only use is jumping between that song's sections, and ↑/↓ and Space reach them as well, one step at a time. A phone, where most of the scrolling happens, has no way to open the overview anyway. Keeping it but limiting it to the song's own column would have meant patching Reveal's overview layout and click handling; switching it off is Reveal's own option, and covers Esc, O and any other way in at once. It is closed before the switch because Reveal's `deactivate()` does nothing once `overview` is false: one left open would have stayed open for the whole song, on a follower with no keyboard to close it.
