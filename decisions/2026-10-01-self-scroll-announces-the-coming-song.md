## 2026-10-01 — Self-scroll announces the coming song to the room

**Context:** In guest mode every client gets an invite dialog, so the audience knows a session has started and which song is next. In self mode — the presenter scrolls — clients were snapped into following silently, with no signal that a session had begun or what was being played.

**Decision:** When the presenter picks „Ich selber möchte scrollen", every client that is present as the session opens is shown „Der nächste Song ist «Song». Es geht gleich los!" with an OK button, once per session, and then follows along. A client that joins mid-session just follows, with no dialog. The presenter sees none.

**Reasoning:** It mirrors the guest invite so both modes announce themselves — but self mode has nothing to volunteer for, so it's an acknowledgement (OK), not a choice. The opening broadcast carries `fresh: true`; the 2 s heartbeats that repeat the session do not. A client only announces on a `fresh` message it hasn't seen (`announcedFor`, keyed by the session id), so the heartbeat never reopens it and a latecomer — who missed the opening and only hears heartbeats — just follows, exactly as a latecomer skips the guest invite once a scroller is named. „Es geht gleich los!" would in any case be wrong for someone arriving mid-song. It never blocks following: the deck moves under the dialog, so dismissing it is optional. The presenter is excluded because it is the one scrolling. Builds on [live-scroll sessions, presenter delegates to a guest](2026-09-30-live-scroll-sessions-presenter-delegates-to-a-guest.md).
