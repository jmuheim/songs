## 2026-10-04 — Ending a session repeats its broadcast to survive one dropped packet

**Context:** Companion fix to [the presenter self-release bug](2026-10-04-presenter-must-not-self-release-while-following-its-own-delegate.md), from the same field-testing report of the presenter struggling to end a live-scroll session. The multiplex relay is a pure fire-and-forget broadcast (`sha256(secret) == socketId`, forward, no storage, no acknowledgment — see the Multiplex section of [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)). Every *ongoing* piece of state — the session, the scroller's position — is safe against a single dropped packet because it is simply repeated on the next 2s heartbeat. `endSession`'s `active: false` message was the one exception: sent exactly once, and by design nothing heartbeats it again afterwards, since from the presenter's point of view there is no more session left to repeat. On a flaky connection, a dropped `active: false` packet meant the rest of the room never heard the session had ended — they would stay locked until each client's own three-heartbeat silence timeout freed it roughly six seconds later, which could easily read as "ending the session didn't do anything."

**Decision:** `endSession` now sends the same `active: false` message three times — immediately, then again at +400ms and +1000ms — before clearing its own local state. The local effects (clearing `session`, navigating to the TOC, flipping the button, showing the toast) still happen once, right away; only the *outgoing* broadcast is repeated.

**Reasoning:** A full acknowledgment/retry protocol would be the thorough fix, but the relay's whole design is deliberately store-nothing and stateless (see the Multiplex section), and this project's one dropped-message failure mode is narrow enough not to need one: everything else already self-heals via the heartbeat, so the fix only needs to extend that same "repeat it a few times" idea to the one message type that currently doesn't get it. Three sends over one second costs nothing on a healthy connection and meaningfully improves the odds on a flaky one, without touching the relay or adding new message types. It does not fully solve a sustained outage (if the connection is down for the whole second, all three attempts are lost) — that residual case still falls back to the three-heartbeat self-release every other client already has, which is an acceptable floor since it was the *only* mechanism before this fix. Tested in [index_html_spec.rb](../spec/browser/index_html_spec.rb) with a spy socket counting how many `active: false` messages arrive after a single click of „Live-Scrollen beenden".
