## 2026-10-03 — Chord tooltip shows a ukulele fingering too

**Context:** The chord tooltip (`style/chords.js`) only ever showed a guitar tab (`|E A D G B e|`), either from a song's own Instructions legend or the built-in `DEFAULT_CHORDS` fallback. Asked to add the matching ukulele fingering (standard GCEA soprano tuning) to the same tooltip.

**Decision:** Add a separate `UKULELE_CHORDS` dictionary (same chord names as `DEFAULT_CHORDS`) and always render its entry, when one exists, as a second `|G C E A|` column beside the guitar tab, labelled "Guitar:"/"Ukulele:" — no click or toggle needed to see it. The two run side by side, not stacked: a slide has more spare width than height, and a landscape viewport wastes that width if the two tabs sit one above the other. The chord name is printed only once, on the guitar column's `Name |…|` row — the ukulele column repeats just `|…|`, not the name again, since the two columns are read together as one fingering, not two independent ones. There is no per-song override for the ukulele voicing: songs' Instructions legends only ever document guitar fingerings, so `UKULELE_CHORDS` is the sole source. A chord with no entry (a custom per-song voicing, or a chord the dictionary doesn't cover) falls back to a single guitar-only column — it does not print a "no fingering" message the way the guitar lookup does for an entirely unknown chord.

**Reasoning:** "Always show both" was chosen over a toggle/second click because the two tabs together are short enough to fit the existing tooltip without crowding it, and a toggle would hide information most people asking for this feature want at a glance. Side by side rather than stacked was a direct correction after seeing the stacked version rendered — it left a lot of unused horizontal space on a slide (reveal.js, landscape by default) while pushing the tooltip taller than it needed to be. No per-song override exists because the song format gives authors no way to write a ukulele voicing (Instructions blocks are a 6-column guitar tab format) — adding one would be speculative scope beyond what was asked. Omitting the column (rather than an explicit "kein Ukulele-Griffbild hinterlegt" message) avoids noise on every custom/non-standard chord a song's Instructions block defines (bass-note variants, riff-specific voicings), which would otherwise show that message far more often than the existing guitar-side message does.

The fingerings themselves came from [ukulele-chords.com](https://ukulele-chords.com), cross-checked against the raw page HTML rather than an AI summary of it — an early summarisation pass hallucinated a wrong fingering for D (reported "1230" instead of the page's actual "2220"), which is why the dictionary comment in `style/chords.js` notes the raw-page cross-check.
