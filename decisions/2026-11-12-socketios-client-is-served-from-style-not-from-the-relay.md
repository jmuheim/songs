## 2026-11-12 — socket.io's client is served from style/, not from the relay

**Context:** The song book is meant to keep working on a slow connection, or none: once loaded, everything is in the page. One thing was not. socket.io's client was loaded from the relay's host (`https://multiplex.up.railway.app/socket.io/socket.io.js`), as a synchronous script, on every page load, in a session or not. The relay sends it with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=0`, so every load asks a second host again, with its own DNS lookup and TLS handshake. On a slow connection `chords.js`, `toc-filter.js` and `wishes.js`, which come after it, waited for that answer. And when the relay was out of reach as the page loaded, the whole multiplex part never started (its `load` handler returns without `window.io`): 📡 did nothing at all, not even open its dialog, so no one could host or join until a reload with a connection.

**Decision:** `style/socketio/socket.io.js` holds the client, byte for byte the file the relay serves (socket.io 2.5.0, the version `multiplex-server/`'s pinned reveal-multiplex brings), with its `LICENSE`. The page loads it from there like qrcodejs. The relay is reached only by the socket, which only a device in a session opens.

**Reasoning:** Loading the client only on joining a session was the other option. It would have saved the 20 KB (gzipped) for a device that never joins, but made `join()` asynchronous, and with it whatever uses the socket right after joining (the host's first heartbeat, the wishes' first round). Worse, it would have needed the network at the moment of joining just for a script, which a later service worker could not serve from its cache unless it had been loaded once anyway. Loaded with the page, the client is there whenever the page is, and socket.io reconnects on its own once the connection comes back. The cost is keeping the client in step with the server by hand: an upgrade of `multiplex-server/` must bring its `dist/socket.io.js` along (see CLAUDE.md, Vendored reveal.js). The structure spec in `index_html_spec.rb` fails on any script that doesn't come from the song book's own origin.
